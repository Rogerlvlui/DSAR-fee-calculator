## Accomplishments

- `apps/web/src/domain/attestation.ts`'s `renderAttestation()`: PRD §9.5's wording transcribed verbatim, with an explicit "not yet recorded" phrase filling the `{reviewedOn}` slot for this pack's null `reviewedOn` rather than an empty gap or a fabricated date, and the past-review-date sentence appended only when the pack's own, uncoerced effective status (`@dsar/rulepacks/stale`'s `deriveEffectiveStatus`) is `STALE`.
- `signOff()` (`apps/web/src/server/data/assessments.ts`) + `POST /api/assessments/{id}/signoff`: refuses at the first failure among matter-not-in-legal-review, any pending line, a missing required acknowledgement, an unknown acknowledged flag, an unaffirmed attestation, or an empty qualification — writing nothing on any refusal. Only on success does it call `computeFinalAmount()` (the only write of `Totals.finalAmount` in `apps/web/src/server/`) and transition the matter to `SIGNED_OFF`.
- `SignOffPanel`/`SignOffPanelView`: the four decision counts, the final figure, one acknowledgement row per matter flag requiring it, the attestation in full, and the primary CTA — disabled with a blocking reason until every acknowledgement is checked, split the same pure/stateful way `DispositionControls` is.
- `apps/web/src/domain/info-request.ts`'s `InfoRequest` + `AssessmentRepository`'s three new methods (`listInfoRequests`/`appendInfoRequest`/`clearInfoRequests`) over a new `demoStore.infoRequests` map — the first extension of that interface since Plan 01.
- `askHr()` + `POST /api/matters/{id}/info-request`: records the query, transitions `LEGAL_REVIEW -> INFO_REQUEST`, and touches no disposition. `dispositionLine`/`bulkAcceptLines`/`signOff` all now share `requireAssessmentInLegalReview()`, refusing with a conflict while a matter sits in `INFO_REQUEST`.
- `resubmitAfterInfoRequest()` (`apps/web/src/server/data/matters.ts`) + `POST /api/matters/{id}/resubmit`: re-runs `assessMatter()` and overwrites the **existing** assessment under its **existing** id — the engine's own derived id is discarded — clearing every disposition and leaving exactly one assessment on file.
- `InfoRequestPanel`/`InfoRequestPanelView`: every outstanding query with the line it concerns and the date raised, plus the re-submit control.
- `apps/web/src/domain/notice-draft.ts`'s `buildNoticeDraft()`: renders the pack's `requestorNoticeTemplate` verbatim, or — because the committed SG-PDPA pack's template still carries the `UNAUTHORED` marker — a plain not-yet-authored statement instead of inventing a letter.
- `apps/web/app/(hr)/matters/[id]/page.tsx`: the three-state HR result screen (waiting / information-requested / signed-off), reading status through `getHrMatterStatus()` (which never carries an assessment) and the figure only through the existing refusing read.
- `ResultView`/`NoticeBlock`: the fee headline, struck lines with their pack-authored basis (no lawyer reason — `ResultViewLine` has no field for one), the draft watermark inside the copyable block, the no-email boundary note, and the mark-sent control (`markNoticeSent()` records a date only, in a small file-local ledger — neither `Matter` nor `Assessment` has a field for it).
