<objective>
Complete the walking skeleton's read surface — one route per role-scoped area, all three reading under the restricted role — and stand up the three self-extending harnesses that every later phase inherits: the route registry cross-check, the role-by-route matrix asserting on bodies, and the glob-driven multipart check.

Purpose: Success Criterion 4 requires the AC-05 and AC-06 tests to enumerate generated artifacts so routes added in Phases 3 through 8 are covered without anyone editing the test, and the roadmap names ACCESS-08 as a standing obligation every later phase inherits. A hand-maintained list rots the moment a phase adds a route and forgets; a glob alone cannot say what roles a route should admit. The answer is both, cross-checked, with the disagreement failing loudly in either direction.

Output: two further role-scoped reads and three standing test harnesses that fail when a future route escapes them.
</objective>
