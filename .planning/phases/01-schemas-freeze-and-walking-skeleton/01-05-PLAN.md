Purpose: these are the three entities that carry every field where the personal-data boundary is enforced at field level. The freeze is transcription — §6.1 and §6.2 name every field, and the discipline here is to add no field and widen no cap.

Output: three compiling zod modules with inferred types and tests pinning each invariant and each cap.
</objective>
