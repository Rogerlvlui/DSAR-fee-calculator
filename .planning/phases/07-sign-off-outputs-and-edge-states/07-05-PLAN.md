<objective>
Add the two missing legal-review-screen surfaces and close ACCESS-05's placement half.

`07-UI-SPEC.md`'s scope note names the stale-pack review notice as a genuinely missing piece:
`packIsStale` is already computed inside `signOff()` for the attestation's extended wording, but
nothing ever renders it as a banner, so a lawyer reviewing under a pack past its review date is
never told before they commit. AC-39's in-flight-review half is the other side of the same
requirement Phase 3 delivered the derived status for.

The download trigger is the visible half of 07-01's endpoint, and it carries this phase's single
highest-value access-control decision: the internal record contains `lawyerReason` and the
internal rationale, so the trigger belongs on `/legal/*` and nowhere else — enforced as a proven
absence, not as a convention.

Purpose: a lawyer must see the pack's review state before signing, and must be able to take the
record away afterwards, without ever creating an HR-reachable path to privileged content.

Output: a full-width red review notice, a secondary download action that appears only after
sign-off, and a boundary proof that no HR screen offers either the trigger or the rationale.
</objective>
