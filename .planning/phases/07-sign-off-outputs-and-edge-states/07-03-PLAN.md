<objective>
Discharge the HR-facing half of this phase: the result screen, the notice text, and the two
gaps a direct read found in the already-built mechanism.

`07-UI-SPEC.md`'s scope note and `07-RESEARCH.md` both confirm `ResultView`, `NoticeBlock` and
`buildNoticeDraft` were built in Phase 3.1/4 and carry forward unchanged — SIGNOFF-05/06 and
DOC-02/03/04 are Pending only because a demo does not discharge a requirement its home phase
owns. Two things are genuinely missing rather than merely unproven: `NoticeBlock` renders the
DRAFT watermark unconditionally, so it persists after the notice is marked sent, which DOC-02's
"watermarked DRAFT until HR marks it sent" forbids; and DOC-03's AC-17 regex assertion does not
exist anywhere.

Purpose: HR must read a figure, a named lawyer, what was allowed and what was struck with the
pack's own plain-language basis, and a notice draft that is provably free of legal reasoning —
and must be able to record that they sent it themselves, without the tool ever suggesting it
did.

Output: a watermark that stops at the right moment, a date-only sent record, an AC-17 scan
over every reachable notice variant, and a zero-allowable outcome proven end to end through the
real route stack as an ordinary result.
</objective>
