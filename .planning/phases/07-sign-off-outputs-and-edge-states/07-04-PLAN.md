<objective>
Expand the tracer's one-page skeleton into the full internal fee assessment record DOC-01 and
DOC-05 describe, without re-deriving a single figure.

07-01 proved the architecture: a workspace package, a pure view module, a `@react-pdf/renderer`
document, a role-gated binary Route Handler, and a footer. What is still missing is the
content — the dense one-row-per-line table D-04 settles on, the matter details and rule pack
sections, the matter flags and their acknowledgements, and the sign-off block with the
attestation as it was actually recorded.

Purpose: a DPO or an auditor opening this file must be able to reconstruct, line by line, which
rule the engine applied, on what stated basis and citation, what the lawyer then decided and
why — and see the named lawyer and the wording they attested to, on a document whose every page
states which pack and which engine produced the figures.

Output: a complete, paginating, greyscale record whose every field comes from the already-stored
assessment.
</objective>
