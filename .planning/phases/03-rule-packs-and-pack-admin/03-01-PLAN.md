<planner_decisions>
Two design forks that research (03-RESEARCH.md Assumption A1, Pitfall 4) left open are closed here, both in favour of the locked decision over the research recommendation.

**PD-1 — D-18 is honoured by a new parallel schema, not by loosening the frozen one.** Research recommended widening `RulePackConstraintsSchema`'s boolean fields to `.nullable()` in place. D-18 lists exactly that as a *rejected* alternative ("re-opens a freeze mid-phase while Phases 4 and 5 run concurrently against it, and widening a required field is not obviously additive") and states its own resolution: "`StubConstraints` is a new type in `packages/schemas`". The locked decision governs. `StubConstraintsSchema` is added alongside `RulePackConstraintsSchema`; nothing frozen is renamed, retyped or removed; `packages/engine` still sees `pack.constraints` as all-boolean and needs no change.

**PD-2 — the frozen schema *is* the ACTIVE gate for constraints.** Because `z.boolean().nullable()` accepts `true`, `false` and `null`, `StubConstraintsSchema` is a strict superset of the frozen shape. So the loader parses every pack — stub and authored alike — with one permissive schema (`LoadableRulePackSchema`), and the ACTIVE-readiness check (plan 03-03) narrows by re-parsing with the untouched frozen `RulePackSchema`, which rejects any surviving `null`. D-18's "a pack carrying any `null` constraint cannot reach ACTIVE" is therefore enforced by the frozen schema itself rather than by a hand-written eleven-key check that could drift from it.
</planner_decisions>

<assumption_delta_decision>
The deterministic assumption-delta probe fired `pluralization` on this phase's scope. The core assumption that has lost its monopoly is real and is not the one the probe's snippet quoted: **"a rule pack's constraints are fully determined"** was true while every pack was an authored pack, and stops being true the moment seven unauthored stubs exist (D-18).

- **Primary noun:** the *loadable* rule pack — a pack whose constraints may or may not have been researched.
- **Decision: `promote`.** `LoadableRulePackSchema` becomes the primary shape the loader returns for all nine packs. The fully-determined `RulePackSchema` is demoted to the narrowed, ACTIVE-ready variant the engine consumes — one variant of the general shape, not the general shape itself. It is deliberately *not* add-alongside: a loader that picked between two pack schemas by reading `status` off unvalidated JSON would reintroduce the singular assumption at the exact point it was supposed to be gone.
- **Companion invariant (accepted, authored as Task 1's acceptance criterion):** every committed pack in the tree round-trips through the primary load path, for every jurisdiction. That test goes red the instant a future phase re-points the loader at `RulePackSchema`.
</assumption_delta_decision>
