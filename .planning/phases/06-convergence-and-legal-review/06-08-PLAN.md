Build keyboard-first navigation for the legal review screen and `/legal/queue` from scratch — no keyboard handling of any kind exists anywhere in this codebase today — plus the one shared, hand-rolled, WCAG-compliant shortcut-help overlay both screens use.

Purpose: 06-RESEARCH.md is explicit that "extending the existing shortcut help overlay" (NAV-03's own wording) is inoperative — there is nothing to extend, only to build. Output: a pure `keyboard-shortcuts.ts` domain module; one shared `ShortcutHelpOverlay`; a `ReviewKeyboardScope` wrapping the review screen's line list; a `QueueKeyboardScope` wrapping `/legal/queue`'s rows — neither scope modifies `disposition-controls.tsx`'s own internals, both drive it through its already-existing `data-action` DOM hooks.
</objective>
