<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Lawyer's flat-fee choice -> `Assessment.feeChoice` | A choice that can only ever REDUCE or hold steady the eventual fee (itemised vs. a flat figure already proven not to exceed it) — never raise it; the server-side block on an exceeding flat figure is the one guarantee this boundary must never regress |
| Recompute confirmation -> discarding every recorded disposition | The single most destructive action this phase adds; must never be reachable via a single click, and must never silently succeed without the lawyer having seen exactly what will be lost |
